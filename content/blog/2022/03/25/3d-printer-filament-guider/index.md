**Problem Statement** -Filament guider for 3D printer PLA –**Scope of work**

– Printing the filament guider in standard size

**Challenges**

–    Removing the support filament from the hollow portion

**Execution Strategy** -Use the correct filing tool

**Value Added** – It’s only a 10-15 min print but so helpful if you want to guide your filament. Just put a standard sized 1.75mm Capricorn tube in and it holds perfectly.
